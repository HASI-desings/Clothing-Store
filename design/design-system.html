Signature elements

Aperture, notch, lattice, notation

Four recurring elements make one system. Each has places it lives and places it must never go.

1. Apertures: hero and featured images only

Band-collar kurtaRs 8,450

2. The notch

Product cells stay rectangles so the wall stays easy to shop. A small notch is cut into the bottom edge, and it widens on hover. The notch also marks the primary button, selected sizes, section labels and toasts.

Never on every card corner, icons, body text, or more than one per control.

3. Lattice lightTHE COURTYARD

Lattice light drifts slowly (26 s loop) and appears at most once per view: the home entrance, an exhibition title or the Studio table. It stops for reduced motion and stays behind text at decorative contrast only.

4. Pattern notation

Fabric facts are set like a tailor's marks inside a dashed cut-line, and every term is explained in plain words. The notation face appears only here, on Studio slot labels and in the menu piece labels.

Layout, edges, motion

The jali module

Grid

12 columns from 1200 px (24 gap, 48 gutter), 8 columns from 768 px (20 gap, 32 gutter), 4 on mobile (16 gap, 16 gutter). Content max 1280, full-bleed max 1440.

Spacing is 4-based: 4, 8, 12, 16, 24, 32, 48, 64, 96, 128, 160. Sections are 64–160 apart.

Breakpoints are designed at 390, 768, 1024 and 1440.

Edges

0 radius for apertures, sheets and buttons (cut paper). 2 px for inputs and swatches. Round only for the bag count and colour dots.

No card shadows. One shadow exists: the raking-light cast under a Studio piece being dragged.

Motion tokens

Cut: cubic-bezier(.2,.7,.2,1). Apertures and sheets.

Assemble: cubic-bezier(.32,0,.12,1). Pieces meeting at a notch.

Durations: 120 micro, 240 UI, 480 assemble, 700 reveal, 26 s drift. Nothing bounces. Everything stops for reduced motion.

Added to bag: Band-collar kurta, M

Do

  • Keep one memorable thing per view: an aperture or lattice light, never both at full strength.
  • Always show price and sizes in stock on product cells.
  • Take colours from tokens; brand colours can be overridden in Settings.

Don't

  • Sale badges in red, urgency colours, countdown banners.
  • Notation font for general labels or eyebrows.
  • Sandstone as a button, link or background colour.
